# AELUNE MVP specification

The explicit integrated plan approved2026-10-11 supersedes the earlier separate beauty/skin flow and three-preview limit. Primary `/kiosk`: age band → distinct unticked purposes → automatically opened camera/local stable-face cancellable3s capture → combined palette/face/base review → skin-first complete report. Support en/ko/es preserving state without AI calls; no required skin/style questionnaire, unknown skin type. Separate cosmetic matcher handles reviewed same-store lip/blush/eye/foundation/concealer; approximate bases require confirmation and concealer brightening explicit selection. Local geometric eye/nose/lip percentages have quality gates and no confidence/attractiveness interpretation. Optional animal vibe is playful appearance only, omitted when uncertain.

The following sections describe preserved legacy skin/personal-color paths and foundation decisions. ## Accepted product decisions

AELUNE (working brand; internal Senmiva identifiers, formerly SMA Skin AI) is a B2B beauty discovery SaaS. An organization has stores, staff memberships, shared products and store-specific listings. Customers use a tablet kiosk or browser-based smart mirror. Staff manage it on a computer. UI language is English. Currency is USD for this release.

The kiosk has one primary task per step: welcome, temporary-session consent, skin type, cosmetic goals, sensitivity/exclusions, optional camera or image selection, visible photo observations, ingredient-to-product matches, finish and clear. On 2026-10-11 the user explicitly changed the active provider to OpenAI photo analysis instead of ConvNeXt. Main session consent and a separate initially unticked adult/single-subject external-photo choice are required before camera access or image transmission. Declining photos runs questionnaire/store-policy matching without opening the camera. With photo consent, one centered face held stable starts a visible three-second countdown, then capture and analysis continue automatically. Losing alignment cancels the countdown; pause, photo selection and photo-free continuation remain available. This is presence/position assistance, never identity recognition or proof of image quality. Customers do not create accounts. The independent skin flow has no saved-result QR; seven-day QR saving belongs to the beauty book. Neither flow includes guest email, profiles, diagnosis or individual customer history. Local experimental inference remains an explicit optional provider; mock remains an explicit demonstration/test provider. Provider selection is a product decision, not an accuracy finding from the unperformed independent comparison.

The staff workspace contains overview counts, searchable product catalog, manual drafts, CSV preview/import, publication, store price/stock/visibility, device pairing/revocation and read-only operating configuration. Owners manage organization products. Managers edit only assigned store listings and devices. Viewers see aggregates. Staff never view customer photos, questionnaires or individual results.

Business owners can sign up with an email and password, confirm an email code and create a company plus first store. The database generates a new isolated workspace atomically with a visibly mock ingredient policy and empty catalog. Signup metadata never grants authority over an existing workspace. Existing-workspace invitations, recovery and membership administration are separate features.

## Recommendation contract

Selected concerns map through a reviewed, versioned organization policy to canonical INCI ingredients. In active OpenAI mode, only a `usable` photo with a clearly `visible` feature may add a bounded cosmetic matching goal: pores → texture, spots → even-looking tone, fine lines → fine lines. These goals are unioned with the guest's selected concerns; selected skin type, sensitivity preference and exclusions remain unchanged. Redness/flaking are displayed without inferring sensitivity, hydration or treatment need. Limited, unassessable, uncertain, not-visible and photo-free observations add no goals. Products qualify only inside the verified device's organization/store, with published complete recognized formulations, visibility, positive stock and no excluded ingredient. OpenAI does not select ingredients, products, concentrations or safety advice. Policy relevance ranks eligible products; skin-type fit and SKU remain deterministic tie breaks. Return at most three products with ingredient reasons. Ingredient presence cannot establish concentration, efficacy or allergy safety.

Optional legacy experimental mode intersects saved recommendation-model candidates with reviewed policy and uses candidate-weighted ranking. Its photo-informed ranking uses the structural source-code contract: all eight predicted codes must form an exact whole category witnessed in retained ingredient TRAIN profiles. Unseen combinations retain questionnaire-only scores. This preserves the original normalization/hash, without inferring clinical need or mapping OpenAI enums to source codes. Relative model outputs are not calibrated confidence or safety probabilities. These code and ingredient models do not run in OpenAI mode.

Product registration does not require AI retraining. Unknown ingredient names may be kept as unresolved draft records. They block publication. CSV must pass all rows before a single transactional draft import; it never auto-publishes. A published formulation is immutable; editing creates/reviews a draft version. Store listing state is independent of formulation publication.

Manual entry allows a blank SKU for new products; the server assigns a persistent internal code. Existing product identity stays fixed and CSV requires explicit SKUs for repeatable imports. The form and HTTP adapter share domain field limits and show specific validation feedback without clearing entered values. Plain ingredient lists preserve chemical commas such as `1,2-Hexanediol`. Supported pasted reports extract names for explicit review, with warnings for ambiguous or missing entries. Source grades, benefits and formulation-purpose descriptions are not stored as ingredients or evidence; extraction never marks the complete formula verified.

The former optional post-ranking hosted explanation remains available only in mock/experimental modes with its separate consent and bounded ingredient/matching facts. OpenAI photo mode suppresses that option and endpoint: skin observations appear before products, even when no product qualifies, with local reviewed matching reasons below. See [OPENAI-SKIN-SETUP.md](OPENAI-SKIN-SETUP.md) for current operation and [LLM-SETUP.md](LLM-SETUP.md) for the preserved explanation subsystem.

Completed OpenAI consultations include a derived `skinConsultation` read model (`skin-consultation-v1`). Its professional appearance summary explains the saved five-feature observations, selected/photo/both care priorities and bounded recommendation use. The kiosk presents this summary first, expandable observation interpretations second, up to six policy-backed ingredient recommendations with cosmetic-purpose and matching rationales next, then up to three eligible store-registered products with their actual formulation reasons. An ingredient can remain recommended when the current catalog has no eligible product containing it; the interface states this clearly and creates no placeholder product. Initial onboarding guide associations remain visibly described as examples requiring merchant review. No-photo, limited and unassessable states explain their limits and use selected goals only.

This report is assembled by the application from validated saved OpenAI observations, the applicable store policy, questionnaire and eligible registered-product reasons. It does not make a second LLM request, transmit additional data, re-analyze the photo, change ranking or persist generated prose. The original photo analysis itself is performed by the OpenAI vision API; report wording is a deterministic interpretation of those saved observations. Current catalog ineligibility removes affected products without inventing new replacements.

## Analysis and privacy contract

Active `openai` mode uses pinned `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` through the private FastAPI provider and fixed Responses endpoint. Main notice is `2026-10-openai-v1`; separate photo notice is `2026-10-openai-photo-v1`. Static JPEG/PNG/WebP input is bounded to 10MiB, 25 million decoded pixels and at least 224px per edge. The service re-encodes oriented RGB pixels as metadata-free JPEG with a 1536px maximum edge/4MiB outbound bound. Only that photo reaches OpenAI, without questionnaire, identity, tenant, product, ingredient or source-training records. The strict five-feature protocol returns redness, spots, pores, fine lines and flaking as `visible|not_visible|uncertain|not_assessable`, plus bounded quality flags. It contains no free-form diagnosis, code grades, probability bars or inferred hydration/sensitivity. `not_visible` means absent from this image's observations, not absent from the person.

An atomic durable per-session claim precedes the photo request. There is at most one external photo attempt, including timeout, refusal, unknown outcomes and process restart; no automatic retry or fallback to ConvNeXt/mock. Completed observations reload without another external call. Failures leave questionnaire-only continuation or finish/new-session choices. A photo-free completion can supersede an outstanding attempt; late results cannot overwrite it. Photo-free calls skip OpenAI entirely. Results, consent/attempt metadata, feature enums and quality flags use normalized transient tables; no pixels, raw provider response or arbitrary output text are persisted. OpenAI requests use `store:false`, no history/tools/retries and a 30-second maximum provider deadline. This does not mean zero provider retention: default abuse-monitoring logs may be kept up to 30 days, subject to documented exceptions. API data is not used for training by default. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)

The isolated research CLI remains available for a separately consented independent ConvNeXt/OpenAI comparison; it has its own private workspace, consent/hash/source/retention gates and no production-provider promotion. No real evaluation photo is currently available; no cross-model accuracy/winner was established. See [VISION-COMPARISON.md](VISION-COMPARISON.md).

Preserved optional providers remain explicit: mock never uploads browser photos and visibly simulates observations; experimental sends bounded images to private local saved ConvNeXt/ingredient models after its own current notice. Its private immutable source-code support artifact is required and checked at startup. No source image/profile row join or clinical interpretation is invented. Missing fields and actual code use/omission remain disclosed. Neither provider is a silent fallback. No customer data is used for training.

Optional experimental results remain normalized transient records with source-code/candidate provenance; their original checkpoints and support artifact stay outside source/deployment images. No weights are loaded, downloaded or trained for OpenAI mode; only a Python API/image runtime is required. New OpenAI relations and contracts are introduced by migrations `202610110004`/`202610110005`. See [DATABASE.md](DATABASE.md) and, for the preserved experimental provider, [ML-SOURCE-CODE-CONTRACT.md](ML-SOURCE-CODE-CONTRACT.md). Staff cannot read individual observations in any mode. Production still needs trusted private ingress, shared quotas and validated operating practices.

Consent is required before questionnaire submission or camera access. Finish/decline/revoke clears transient records. The kiosk warns after 90 seconds and resets at 120 seconds without interaction. Abandoned server sessions expire after 15 minutes, and Supabase requires scheduled cleanup. Page exit attempts a best-effort finish; expiry is the fallback. Only store/day counters remain. No request-body logging or customer identifiers in analytics.

Browser face detection uses pinned, self-hosted MediaPipe CPU runtime/model assets with reviewed Apache-2.0 notices and hash checks. Detection geometry stays transient in the browser. The upstream SDK can attempt utilization metrics; the application's enforced same-origin connection CSP blocks external browser requests even when client navigation enters the kiosk. Server-side private inference/OpenAI calls retain their separate consent boundaries. Uploads bypass live alignment checks and are disclosed as such. Camera permission remains a browser decision; blocked permission or detector failure never captures silently.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new guest sees OpenAI photo observations before products, including empty catalogs, and can use a photo-free alternative and clear the session.
- A completed OpenAI result explains appearance and care priorities professionally, recommends policy-backed ingredients even when products are unavailable, and connects every displayed store product to actual listed ingredients and matching reasons. Source labels distinguish selected goals from usable photo observations; limited/unassessable/no-photo results never invent photo-derived priorities.
- In OpenAI mode, default-off adult/single-subject external consent gates camera/image access. Live capture requires one centered face across fresh frames, shows a cancellable three-second countdown and stops on leave/pause/reset. Declining external consent causes no camera or image request.
- Only usable/visible pores, spots and fine lines may add reviewed cosmetic matching goals; redness/flaking/uncertain/limited results do not imply hydration, sensitivity or a diagnosis. Store eligibility and exclusions remain authoritative.
- At most one external photo attempt occurs per session. Failure offers photo-free completion or a new session; completed cached observations and late/aborted responses cannot create another external call or restore finished data.
- The next guest cannot see the prior photo/answers/results. Late requests cannot recreate a finished consultation.
- A staff owner can register/import drafts, review and publish; a store manager can set price, stock and visibility. Ineligible products cannot be recommended.
- A new business owner can verify their email and create their own company/store exactly once; retries preserve the workspace, and unconfirmed, device or existing inactive staff identities cannot gain authority through setup.
- Two stores and tenants cannot access each other's session/catalog/listing relationships. Anonymous unpaired users cannot access protected RPCs. Staff cannot elevate their role via input.
- Existing tests, type checks, lint and production build pass. Responsive visual and accessibility checks cover landscape/portrait tablet and desktop workspace.
- Repository documentation, lockfiles, setup examples and short task-context workflow let another developer continue without the chat history.

## Scope control

New features are recorded in BACKLOG.md with an acceptance test before implementation. Change a stable decision only when a real requirement or test failure justifies it. Decisions are kept in ARCHITECTURE.md rather than repeatedly redesigning the system.
