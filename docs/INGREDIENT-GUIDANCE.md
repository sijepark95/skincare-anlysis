# Ingredient guidance editorial scope

The professional consultation combines saved OpenAI appearance enums with the guest's selected goals, the exact saved store policy and actual eligible registered formulas. Human-authored explanations are rendered locally; no second LLM request, external questionnaire transfer or new ingredient classifier is involved. Ingredient functions never add an ingredient, change ranking or bypass exclusions. A rule in the applicable published store policy is always required.

Visible pores now name their routed goal **Visible pores & texture**. The ingredient shortlist reserves coverage for each applicable goal before filling its six slots, so hydration ingredients cannot crowd out all texture guidance. Product selection preserves existing eligible scores, reserves the best eligible product for uncovered usable photo goals, then fills the three slots and returns that subset in original rank order. Every goal shows approved-ingredient and recommended-product coverage, or explains the missing policy/product connection. Unknown exclusions, stock, publication, formulas and tenant/store gates remain hard filters. Absence from three recommendations is never described as absence from the whole store. Lip products use the separate confirmed-palette matcher and are excluded here.

`src/domain/ingredient-knowledge.ts` contains a deliberately bounded set of general cosmetic-function descriptions. Unreviewed names receive only their store-policy goal rationale, without an invented scientific benefit. The examples below describe ingredients or studied formulations; they do not establish effectiveness, concentration, allergy safety or suitability of a registered retail product.

Expand only with a traceable primary source and a separate content review. Source links are documentation references rather than calls made by the kiosk.
